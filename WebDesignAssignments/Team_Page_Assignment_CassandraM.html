Meet the Team!


Remy, Head Chef

La Ratatouille's founder and ex Gusteau chef, Remy is the star of the famous rags-to-riches story. His favorite food is cheese.

Colette Tatou, Sous Chef

Also an former Gusteau chef, Colette keeps the employees (Remy's family) in check. Her favorite food is crepes.

Alfredo Linguini, Waiter

Once a fake chef, Linguini now has found his passion bussing tables. He and Colette also manage La Ratatouille. His favorite food is omelettes.

Emile, Taste Tester

Although he is not a picky eater, Emile still has to give his approval to every menu item. He doesn't have a favorite food.
